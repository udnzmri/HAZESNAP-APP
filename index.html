🌍 About HazeSnap!

HazeSnap is a platform that helps communities monitor haze and air pollution. Users can check air-quality information, receive health alerts and report haze incidents in their area. Community reports help improve awareness and support faster action to protect public health.


HazeSnap supports SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being by promoting awareness, prevention, community participation and healthier communities.

😷 Air Pollutant Index (API) Level Guide

Guide to five air quality levels and health protection measures.

1. Good

API 0–50

Pollution: Very low.

Advice: Outdoor activities can continue as usual.

2. Moderate

API 51–100

Pollution: Moderate pollution.

Advice: Sensitive individuals are encouraged to reduce heavy outdoor physical activity.

3. Unhealthy

API 101–200

Pollution: Unhealthy air quality.

Advice: Limit outdoor activities, especially for vulnerable groups.

4. Very Unhealthy

API 201–300

Pollution: Very high haze.

Advice: Avoid outdoor activities and follow official health advice.

5. Hazardous

API >300

Pollution: Extreme pollution level.

Advice: Reduce exposure to outdoor air and seek treatment if experiencing breathing difficulties.
